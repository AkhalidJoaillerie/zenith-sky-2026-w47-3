This wide-field integration captures the celestial zenith above Obour, Egypt, during the early morning hours of 18 November 2026. Pointed directly upward along the local vertical axis, the optical field spans the winter Milky Way corridor across Auriga, Taurus, Perseus, Orion, and Gemini.

Following pre-capture GNSS and inertial sensor lock at 00:34:41 EET, the camera acquired 40 calibrated light frames (10s–12s exposures at ISO 800) between 00:37:18 and 00:47:32 EET (astronomical mid-exposure 00:42:25 EET / 22:42:25 UTC), followed immediately by 10 thermal dark frames between 00:48:42 and 00:51:05 EET at 25.1°C.

By bypassing in-camera noise reduction and edge sharpening in RAW (.dng) mode, linear stellar profiles were preserved prior to statistical alignment in Sequator and tone mapping in RawTherapee. Full inertial sensor logs, astrometric residuals for all 43 solved stars, and calibration steps are documented in the Telemetry & Pipeline record.
